# Boyes Park Redevelopment Project  

## Overview  
This project redevelops **Boyes Park** in Hamilton, New Zealand, with a focus on **safety, accessibility, inclusivity, and enhanced amenities**. It was carried out by Team 3 (*Enhancing Amenities*) in collaboration with Hamilton City Council.  

The work combines principles of:  
- **Safety by Design (CPTED)** – natural surveillance, access control, maintenance, and territorial reinforcement.  
- **Accessibility & Inclusivity** – improved pathways, splash pads, inclusive restrooms, and parking.  
- **Amenities & Community Engagement** – more lighting, seating, rubbish bins, BBQs, and water fountains.  
- **Digital Engagement** – visual posters and an interactive consultation website.

## My Work

As the Computer Science member of the Enhancing Amenities Team 3, my primary responsibilities focused on the technical and digital aspects of the Boyes Park Redevelopment project.

- Webpage Creation: I designed and developed an interactive project website that presented our proposal in an engaging and accessible way. The website allowed community members and stakeholders to explore the park redevelopment plan, view visuals of the proposed facilities, and submit feedback or booking enquiries to support future community-driven improvements.

- Digital Map Design: I created a visual and digital map of Boyes Park highlighting proposed upgrades such as accessible restrooms, lighting, seating, splash pads, and water stations. The map used intuitive icons to make navigation easy and to illustrate how inclusivity and safety were integrated into the design.

- Data & Technical Support: I contributed to data collection, analysis, and visualisation, using structured reasoning and technical tools to present findings from site assessments and surveys.

- Documentation & Visual Tools: To maintain a professional and academic presentation, I used LaTeX to create clean, structured documentation and reports supporting the project’s technical and design outputs.
